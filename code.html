The Fikra Story: From Crucible to Flagship Academy

In 2008, a distinguished circle of educators, astrophysicists, and humanities scholars gathered to confront a widening fissure in modern education: the separation of pure scientific curiosity from ethical character formation. Starting as an experimental youth science crucible known as “The Fikra Circle,” the academy was built to honor classical dialectic discussion while leveraging state-of-the-art empirical research laboratories.
